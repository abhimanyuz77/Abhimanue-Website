Why I Built This

“I wasn't a naturally brilliant student. Until 8th standard, I struggled to study, stay focused, and, honestly, even understand how to learn.

That began to change in 9th standard when I became deeply curious about memory, learning, focus, and how the mind works. I started exploring books, research, and ideas from experts on learning and personal development. Over time, I experimented with different techniques and gradually built my own approach to studying more effectively.

By 10th standard, I had gone from being an average student to becoming a topper. But the biggest lesson wasn't the marks. It was realizing that learning itself is a skill.

Most people are taught what to learn, but very few are taught how to learn. How to focus when your mind keeps wandering. How to remember what you've learned. How to stay consistent without burning out. How to actually know whether you're improving.

That's why I built the Learning Operating System. It isn't another course or a collection of study tips — it's a practical system that tracks your daily process, measures your performance, and guides you with a topper's strategy, whether you're a student, a UPSC, NEET or IIT-JEE aspirant, or anyone struggling to study effectively.

My goal is simple: I don't want you to spend more hours learning. I want you to get more out of every hour you spend.”

Abhimanue Udayan
