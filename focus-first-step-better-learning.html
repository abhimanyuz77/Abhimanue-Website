Focus: The First Step Towards Better Learning

By Abhimanue Udayan October 17, 2026
Video Lesson

Before you learn how to study better, remember more, or plan your day effectively, you need to understand one thing: focus.

Preparing for competitive exams like NEET, JEE, and UPSC requires more than hard work. It requires knowing what matters to you and directing your attention towards it consistently.

In a world full of notifications, social media, gaming, and endless distractions, protecting your attention is becoming increasingly important.

In this video, I share my observations from teaching competitive exam students and explain why focus is the foundation of effective learning.

Watch the video above to understand how you can start taking control of your attention and build better study habits.

Your First Exercise

After watching the video, take a few minutes to:

  • Write down your academic goal
  • Identify the three biggest things stealing your attention
  • Decide one small change you can make to protect your study time

Focus isn't about never getting distracted. It's about learning to bring your attention back to what matters.
