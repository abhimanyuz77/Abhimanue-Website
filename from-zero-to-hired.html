The Problem

You Graduated.
Now What?

A degree was supposed to be the answer. For millions of Indian graduates, it turns out to be only the first question.

You graduated. You have a degree. Perhaps you even have a few certificates.

But where are the jobs? How do you compete with thousands of other graduates? How do you convince a company to hire you when you don't have work experience?

And more importantly, how do you move beyond low-paying opportunities and build a career that actually changes your financial future?

From Zero to Hired is a practical guide designed to help unconventional Indian graduates understand the job market, build employable skills, find opportunities and take control of their careers.

Is This You?

This Book Is For You If…

You have a degree but still don't know how to get your first job.

You are struggling to find opportunities despite applying everywhere.

You feel your educational background is holding you back.

You cannot afford expensive courses and certifications.

You have no work experience and don't know how to build a convincing resume.

You want to work for startups, established companies or international employers.

You want to earn more and build long-term financial independence.

What's Inside

What Will You Learn?

Five parts. One journey — from understanding the game to taking action on it.

I
Part One

Understanding the Game

Understand where you stand before deciding where you want to go.

Discover how India's economic and employment landscape works, why degrees alone aren't enough and how to break out of limited opportunities.

II
Part Two

Building Your Employability

Become someone companies actually want to hire.

Discover valuable skills, learn without spending a fortune, build a portfolio without work experience and demonstrate your abilities through real projects.

III
Part Three

Getting Your First Job

Turn your skills into actual employment opportunities.

Learn to build a compelling resume, optimize your LinkedIn profile, find hidden job opportunities, network without connections and approach companies directly.

IV
Part Four

Climbing the Corporate Ladder

Think beyond your first paycheck.

Explore opportunities in startups, unicorns and international companies. Learn salary negotiation, career progression and how to build financial independence through your career.

V
Part Five

The 30-Day Job Hunting Challenge

Stop preparing endlessly. Start taking action.

Put everything into practice with a personal career readiness assessment, skills gap analysis, daily job search routine, application tracker and a structured 30-day action plan.

The Difference

Why This Book Is Different

Most career advice tells you to work hard, improve your skills and apply for jobs.

But what if you don't know which skills to learn? What if you have no money for expensive training? What if you don't have connections or previous work experience?

This book approaches these problems from the perspective of an unconventional graduate navigating the realities of the Indian employment landscape.

It focuses on practical steps, accessible learning resources, unconventional opportunities and taking action with what you already have.

Practical Steps

Clear, doable actions you can start with today — not vague advice about "working hard".

Accessible Learning

Methods and resources that don't require expensive courses or paid certifications.

Unconventional Opportunities

Paths that typical career advice ignores — startups, direct approaches and international employers.

Action With What You Have

No connections, no experience, no big budget? You can still start from where you are.
