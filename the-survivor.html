Artist Statement

For most of my life, I explored the world through science, engineering, and education.

Physics taught me to search for hidden order.

Engineering taught me to appreciate structure.

Technology taught me to recognize patterns.

Eventually I realized those same principles existed everywhere around us. Especially inside nature.

My artistic practice begins with observation rather than creation. I search for extraordinary natural forms that reveal decades of growth, resilience, and transformation.

I preserve them. Present them. Share their stories.
