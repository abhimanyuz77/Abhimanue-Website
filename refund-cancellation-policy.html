Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy carefully before enrolling in any of our programs

Policy at a Glance

Non-Refundable Programs

All program and course fees are non-refundable once payment is completed. If a refund is exceptionally approved on a customer request, the payment gateway charges and applicable taxes will be deducted from the refunded amount.

1. Non-Refundable Fees

All enrollment and program fees paid to Abhimanue Academy / Hexagon Know are non-refundable. Our programs involve personalized work — consultation scheduling and preparation, diagnostic assessments, personalized study roadmaps, and reserved seats on our learning platform — all of which begin immediately after enrollment.

No refunds, partial refunds, or pro-rata refunds will be issued for:

  • Change of mind after enrollment
  • Non-usage of the course, platform, or learning materials
  • Missed sessions or discontinuation partway through a program
  • Dissatisfaction after significant progress through the course content
  • Accounts that violated our Terms and Conditions or shared login credentials
  • Completed courses, or requests made after the eligibility window described below

2. Exceptional Refund Requests

A refund may be considered, solely at our discretion, in genuine cases raised by the customer, such as:

  • A duplicate payment made for the same program
  • A verified technical failure on our side that prevents access to the program
  • A written request made within 7 days of payment and before your consultation or platform onboarding has begun

Approval of any such request is not guaranteed. Each request is reviewed individually.

3. Payment Gateway Fee Deduction

If a refund is approved on a customer request, the refunded amount will be the fee you paid minus the payment gateway / processing charges levied on the original transaction (typically 2–3% of the transaction amount plus GST) and minus any other non-recoverable charges or applicable taxes.

Payment gateway charges are not returned to us by the payment processor and therefore cannot be refunded to you.

4. How to Request a Refund Review

Contact Support

2

Our team will verify your request and check your eligibility

3

Decision

If approved at our discretion, we'll initiate the refund to your original payment method, minus payment gateway charges

4

Processing (7–10 business days)

The approved refund amount will be credited to your bank account or original payment method

5. Cancellation

You may stop participating in a program at any time by informing us. Cancellation does not entitle you to any refund. If you simply stop attending, your access continues until the end of the paid period but no refund is due. Where a refund is exceptionally approved, your course and platform access will be revoked when the refund is processed.

6. Payment Method for Approved Refunds

UPI / Bank Transfer

Approved refunds will be credited back to your account within 7–10 business days.

Credit / Debit Card

Approved refunds will be credited back to your card within 7–10 business days. Card payment gateway charges (approximately 3%) are never refunded.

7. Special Cases

Program Discontinuation

If Abhimanue Academy discontinues a program before it is delivered, enrolled students will receive a refund for the undelivered portion.

Technical Issues

If you experience persistent technical issues that prevent course access, please contact support first so we can resolve them. If we cannot resolve a verified issue on our side, a refund may be approved at our discretion.

Duplicate Payments

If you've been charged twice for the same program, contact us with both transaction details. Verified duplicate payments will be refunded, minus any non-recoverable payment gateway charges.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I get a refund after enrolling?

A: Program fees are non-refundable. Exceptional requests may be reviewed at our discretion if made within 7 days of payment and before your consultation or platform onboarding has begun.

Q: If a refund is approved, how long does it take?

A: Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to your original payment method.

Q: Why is my refund less than the amount I paid?

A: Approved refunds have the payment gateway charges (typically 2–3% plus GST) and any applicable taxes deducted, because those charges are not returned to us by the payment processor.

Q: Will I lose access to the course after a refund?

A: Yes, your course and platform access will be revoked once the refund is processed.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under applicable law.
